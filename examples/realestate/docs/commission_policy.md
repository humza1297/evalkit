# Harbor Point Realty: Commission Policy

(Fictional sample data for evalkit demos.)

## Listing commission

The standard listing commission is 5 percent of the final sale price. For properties listed above $1,500,000 the commission is 4 percent.

Commission is split 50/50 between the listing brokerage and the buyer's brokerage unless the listing agreement says otherwise.

## Agent splits

New agents in their first year keep 60 percent of the brokerage share. After the first year, agents keep 70 percent. Agents who close more than 20 transactions in a calendar year move to an 80 percent split the following year.

## Referral fees

Harbor Point pays a referral fee of 25 percent of the agent's commission for referrals from other licensed brokerages. Referral agreements must be signed before the client is introduced.

## Disputes

Commission disputes between agents are reviewed by the managing broker, Priya Natarajan, within 10 business days.
