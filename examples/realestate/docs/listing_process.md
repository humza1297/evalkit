# Harbor Point Realty: Listing Process

(Fictional sample data for evalkit demos.)

## Steps to list a home

1. Book a free listing consultation with an agent.
2. The agent prepares a comparative market analysis within 3 business days.
3. Sign the exclusive listing agreement. The standard term is 6 months.
4. Schedule professional photography. Harbor Point covers the cost of photos and a 3D tour.
5. The listing goes live on the MLS within 48 hours of photography.

## Seller disclosures

Sellers must complete the property disclosure form before the listing goes live. Known defects such as roof leaks, foundation issues or past flooding must be disclosed.

## Cancelling a listing

A seller may cancel the listing agreement with 30 days written notice. If the seller cancels within the first 60 days, a $500 marketing fee applies.

## Earnest money

Buyer earnest money deposits are held in the Harbor Point trust account and must be deposited within 2 business days of acceptance.
