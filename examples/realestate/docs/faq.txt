Harbor Point Realty: Client FAQ
(Fictional sample data for evalkit demos.)

Do you help first-time buyers?
Yes. We run a free first-time buyer workshop on the first Tuesday of every month at 6pm.

Do you handle rentals?
Yes. Our property management team manages long-term rentals. The management fee is 8 percent of monthly rent, plus a leasing fee of half of one month's rent for each new tenant.

Can I tour a home virtually?
Yes. Every Harbor Point listing includes a 3D tour, and agents can do live video walkthroughs on request.

Which areas do you serve?
We serve Bayview, Old Harbor, Kestrel Heights and the Northshore district.
