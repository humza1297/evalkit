# Harbor Point Realty: Office Handbook

(Fictional sample data for evalkit demos.)

## Hours and contact

The office at 418 Marina Way opens at 9am and closes at 6pm Monday to Friday. Saturday hours are 10am to 2pm. The office is closed on Sundays.

The main office phone number is 555-0140. After-hours emergencies for property management clients go to 555-0199.

## Parking

Clients can park for free in the three spaces marked "Harbor Point Visitor" behind the building. Agents park in the municipal lot on Dock Street.

## Lockboxes and keys

Lockbox codes are issued through the ShowingDesk app and change every 7 days. Physical keys must be signed out at the front desk and returned the same day.

## Document retention

Signed transaction files are kept for 7 years after closing. Files are stored in the secure document system; paper copies are shredded after scanning.
